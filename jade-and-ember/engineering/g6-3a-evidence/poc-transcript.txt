# Release control: a local proof of concept (synthetic clients, disposable tokens, a throwaway folder)

$ rc init-demo --clients alpha,beta
  created <tmp>/rc (clients: alpha, beta)
  disposable tokens: <tmp>/operator/{owner,scheduler,agent}.token
  SYNTHETIC: nothing here is a real credential, repository or host. (2 per-client deploy tokens are in rc/vault/.)

## 1. A coding agent tries to use release control: it has no capability at all

$ rc stage --client alpha --package <prepared package>   [as agent]
  REFUSED [forbidden]: the agent role may not stage

$ rc approve --client alpha --stage st-anything   [as agent]
  REFUSED [forbidden]: the agent role may not approve

## 3. Routine maintenance: automatic only when the owner preauthorised that kind

$ rc run-scheduled --client alpha --package <prepared package>   [as scheduler]
  WAITING FOR THE OWNER: stage st-20261012-2-8b9dacdc is TEXT-ONLY and no preauthorisation covers it
    only the words of 1 page(s) changed (30 characters)
  review page: <tmp>/rc/staged/alpha/st-20261012-2-8b9dacdc/review.html

$ rc preauth-grant --client alpha --kinds identical,text-only --days 30 --max 10   [as owner]
  PREAUTHORISED pa-11: routine identical and text-only releases for alpha, up to 10, for 30 days. Material changes still need you.

$ rc run-scheduled --client alpha --package <prepared package>   [as scheduler]
  RELEASED AUTOMATICALLY st-20261012-3-8b9dacdc (text-only) to deploy/alpha/current: a preauthorisation covered it; digest matched by the engine

    the search subject changed, so a person has to look at it again
    the externalLinks subject changed, so a person has to look at it again
    llms.txt is not a page, so its change is not routine
    order-online.html: a tag, attribute, link, script, style or comment changed
  review page: <tmp>/rc/staged/alpha/st-20261012-4-247baa39/review.html

$ rc auto-release --client alpha --stage st-20261012-4-247baa39   [as scheduler]
  REFUSED [needs-owner]: a material change is never released automatically

$ rc approve --client alpha --stage st-20261012-4-247baa39   [as owner]
  REFUSED [subjects-not-recorded]: still to be recorded by the owner: search, externalLinks

## 5. Things that must not work

A revoked approval stops counting; a package altered after staging is refused; a package for another client is refused; a release cannot be written to another client.

$ rc stage --client alpha --package <prepared package>   [as owner]
  STAGED st-20261012-5-8b9dacdc: MATERIAL
    - the search subject changed, so a person has to look at it again
    - the externalLinks subject changed, so a person has to look at it again

## 6. Controls: freeze, audit, isolation

  FROZEN alpha: nothing can be staged or released until the owner unfreezes it

  REFUSED [frozen]: alpha is frozen: demo: pausing releases

$ rc unfreeze --client alpha --reason demo: resuming   [as owner]
  UNFROZEN alpha
